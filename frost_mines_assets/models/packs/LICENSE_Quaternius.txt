The curated GLBs retain the source geometry and painted materials. Import
processing embedded external resources, applied uniform scale and ground/XZ
normalization, set metallic 0 / roughness 0.92, and reduced texture atlases to
512x512 for sub-cell RTS rendering. See SOURCES.md for exact files and hashes.
