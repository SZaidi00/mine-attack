Direction notes

B+C · Doctrine Deck

A hybrid of the cinematic doctrine map and the card-based research deck: visible branch paths, compact technology cards, selected-node details, and persistent queue controls.

What to evaluate

  • The map preserves branch relationships and exclusive choices from option B.
  • Each node carries option C’s state badge, icon, and compact description.
  • The detail rail and queue controls keep cost, timing, and exclusions readable.

Production fit

  • Options A, B, and C remain available for comparison.
  • The B+C hybrid combines positioned nodes with reusable card components.
  • All options can share the same cost, progress, lock, and queue data.
